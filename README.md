# HobbyLib

**Your Personal Hobby Library & AI-Powered Collection Assistant**

HobbyLib is an AI-powered personal hobby management and collectibles discovery platform for books, manga, figures, toys, anime merchandise, and other collectibles.

It organizes personal collections, maintains a structured product catalog, and automatically monitors Japanese secondhand marketplaces such as Mandarake and Surugaya.

By intelligently identifying products, matching marketplace listings, tracking prices, and discovering rare or missing collectibles, HobbyLib delivers personalized reports, alerts, and purchasing recommendations.

Our Vision: Build an intelligent, self-maintaining hobby library that connects personal interests with the ever-changing collectibles marketplace.
## Key Features

### Collection Management
- Organize items by franchise, series, character, manufacturer, category, and tags.
- Maintain consistent product names and descriptions.
- Identify duplicate entries, different editions, and missing items within a product series.

### AI-Powered Product Intelligence
- Automatically classify and enrich product information.
- Match marketplace listings to existing catalog entries.
- Recognize product aliases across Japanese, English, and Chinese.
- Research newly released products and expand the catalog.

### Marketplace Monitoring
- Monitor collectible listings on supported websites such as Mandarake and Surugaya.
- Track items of interest, including Chiyoko-related merchandise.
- Detect new listings, price changes, and availability updates.
- Reduce duplicate notifications and irrelevant search results.

### Personalized Reports & Recommendations
- Generate periodic marketplace reports.
- Highlight rare or newly available collectibles.
- Identify potential collection gaps.
- Recommend items based on personal interests, collection history, and preferences.

## Vision

**HobbyLib aims to become an intelligent, self-maintaining hobby library that continuously learns about my interests, discovers relevant collectibles, and helps me make informed purchasing decisions.**
